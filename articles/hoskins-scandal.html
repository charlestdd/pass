HOSKINS

For years, Andrew Hoskins has occupied a peculiar position in Godfall's upper class. At twenty-eight, he is young enough to remain a fixture of celebrity culture and wealthy enough to move comfortably among the world's most powerful families.

His official fortune of approximately 910 million IC places him among the world's richest individuals. His public image has been carefully constructed around legitimate business interests, luxury and social prominence.

"The question is no longer simply who Andrew Hoskins is. It is how many people have been protecting the structure around him."

THE DELUNT CONNECTION

Before Andrew Hoskins became a legitimate businessman, he was closely associated with Jake Delunt, the convicted former leader of the Delunt Mafia.

Hoskins served as one of Delunt's closest associates before the organization eventually collapsed.

THE DREW WHITLEY FILE

The most unusual discovery concerns a man named Drew Whitley.

Approximately 1 billion IC is associated with the name Drew Whitley.

But Whitley was not simply a fictional identity. He was Andrew Hoskins's deceased brother.

THE VANCE-ROMANO NETWORK

Hoskins later became a major partner of the Vance-Romano family, operating as a broker and supervisor within networks established by the family.

THE SENIOR PRINCE CONNECTION

The political sensitivity surrounding the investigation is heightened by Vance-Romano's relationship with Darius Shacklebolt, Senior Prince of UNC.

WHY THE IEJ HAS STRUGGLED

The International Enforcement of Justice has maintained interest in Hoskins for years, but proving that an individual is the organizer of a criminal network is considerably more difficult when the network has been deliberately divided among independent intermediaries.
